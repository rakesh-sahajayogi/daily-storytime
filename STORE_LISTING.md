## Full description
Little Lantern is a gentle, parent-led storytime app. Each day brings a short original story sized for reading aloud—no accounts, no ads, no feeds.

Features:
• Story of the day for an easy nightly ritual
• Illustrated page-by-page reader
• Optional on-device voice narration
• Favorites and a soft reading streak
• Bedtime mode with a calmer night palette
• Parent zone behind a grown-up check

Made for shared moments: you hold the phone, your child hears the story.
