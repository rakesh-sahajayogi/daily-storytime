# Privacy Policy — Little Lantern

**Last updated:** August 13, 2026

Little Lantern (“the App”) is a parent-led children’s story reading app.

## What we collect

Little Lantern does **not** collect personal information, create accounts, show ads, or use analytics/tracking SDKs.

On your device only, the App may store:

- Favorite story IDs
- Completed story IDs and reading streak dates
- Bedtime mode preference

This data stays in on-device storage (AsyncStorage) and is not uploaded to our servers. We do not operate a cloud backend for this App.

## Voice narration

Optional “Read aloud” uses your device’s built-in text-to-speech. Audio is generated on-device and is not sent to Little Lantern servers.

## Children

The App is designed for parents/caregivers to read with children. Parent-only settings are protected by a simple parental gate. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal data.

## Third parties

Store distribution (Apple App Store / Google Play) is governed by those platforms’ own policies when you download the App.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the project repository or email the developer listed on the store listing.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the App’s data practices change. The “Last updated” date will be revised accordingly.
